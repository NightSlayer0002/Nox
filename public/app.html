Glow and studio help
Gesture guide
A gentle poke
A smile, sometimes squeezed >< eyes.
Too many pokes
Annoyed eyes and a tiny frown. Every seventh separate burst gets a rare, big-eyed cute outrage.
Pick him up
Wide eyes looking where he might land. Hold too long and he worries.
Shake while holding
Fast left/right or up/down reversals give him spiral eyes. He recovers after a few seconds.
Let go
A relieved smile, or a fall when Gravity is on. Hard landings get an ouch and a squash.
Come a little closer
Linger by his face for a shy smile.
Give him a quiet moment
After 24 seconds he yawns, then dozes. Move the cursor to wake him.
Set the scene
Starry eyes for Orbit, a smirk for Takeover, worry for Echo.
